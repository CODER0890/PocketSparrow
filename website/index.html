About Project

Why Pocket Sparrow?

Traditional cybersecurity and anti-phishing tools send your URLs, message snippets, or telemetry up to cloud servers (e.g., Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal). This creates two major problems:

Problem 1: Privacy Leakage

Private internal URLs, confidential tokens, and personal communication snippets leave your device and get indexed on third-party servers.

Problem 2: Network Latency & Dead Zones

Cloud lookups introduce 200ms–1,000ms latency and completely fail when traveling, in remote areas, or in Airplane Mode.

Pocket Sparrow solves this with an air-gapped architecture. By compiling a multi-tiered rulebook (entropy analysis, Cyrillic homoglyph normalizer, regex scoring) with an INT8 post-training quantized neural model directly into the binary, threat evaluation occurs in under 50 microseconds right on your processor with 0 outbound bytes.

Capabilities

Core Protection Modules

Live Shield (Mobile)

Background notification listener scanning WhatsApp, Telegram, and SMS notifications with zero cloud leakage.

Email Phishing Auditor

Offline heuristic inspection for brand impersonation, webmail spoofing, and dangerous attachment extensions.

Quishing Radar

Offline CameraX QR code scanner detecting malicious redirects, high entropy links, and high-risk TLDs.

Process Sentry

Linux /proc and Android package auditing detecting unauthorized reverse shells, rogue APKs, and hidden miners.
